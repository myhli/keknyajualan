# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Indonesian digital creators, developers, students, and professionals looking for verified AI and creative software subscriptions (Google AI Pro 18 Bulan, Gemini Advanced, Canva Pro) directly from a trusted seller via WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

A fast, mobile-first product catalog and instant WhatsApp checkout portal for keknyaJualan digital subscriptions.

## Positioning

Direct, curated inventory catalog with pre-filled product order messages for instant one-tap WhatsApp checkout.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first link-in-bio traffic from social media (Instagram @keknyaJualan, Telegram, TikTok). Visitors view products on their phones in seconds, tap the desired package, and are immediately routed to WhatsApp with their order pre-filled.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pure React Server Component (no client-side hydration waterfall, no "use client").
- Native Node.js `fs` reads `products.json` directly.
- Dynamic WhatsApp URL formatted per product: `https://wa.me/6283144569056?text=Halo%2C%20Saya%20mau%20membeli%20<item>.`.
- Strictly NO AI SLOP: avoid generic template cards, clunky oversized shapes, emojis pretending to be icons, and low-effort neobrutalist copies.

1. Instant Clarity: Visitors know product details, specs, and warranty clearly.
2. Zero Purchase Friction: One tap takes the user directly to a pre-filled WhatsApp conversation with the seller.
3. Boutique Trust: Visuals must look like a curated, high-end digital agency/kiosk, not an automated low-effort template or generic AI slop.
